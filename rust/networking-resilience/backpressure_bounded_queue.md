Bounded FIFO queue that emits a backpressure signal (Go/Stop) with hysteresis.
Push flips to Stop once length reaches the high watermark; pop only flips back
to Go once length falls strictly below the low watermark — the two distinct
thresholds prevent flapping around a single boundary. Use to gate producers
against a slow consumer. Guarantees: state never oscillates within the
[low, high) band; asserts verify the exact Go/Stop transition points.

## Guarantee

When it runs, backpressure bounded queue guarantees assert_eq!(bq.size(), 5, "Queue has 5 items"); assert_eq!(bq.size(), 2, "Queue at low watermark"); assert_eq!(bq.size(), 1, "Queue has 1") (proven by rustc-run).

**Checkable constraints:**

- `assert!(low_watermark < high_watermark)`
- `assert_eq!(bq.state(), BackpressureState::Go, "Initial state is Go")`
- `assert_eq!(state, BackpressureState::Go, "State Go until high watermark")`
- `assert_eq!(state, BackpressureState::Stop, "State Stop at high watermark")`
- `assert_eq!(bq.size(), 5, "Queue has 5 items")`
- `assert_eq!(bq.size(), 2, "Queue at low watermark")`
- `assert_eq!(bq.state(), BackpressureState::Stop, "Still Stop at low watermark")`
- `assert_eq!(bq.size(), 1, "Queue has 1")`
